# MboaPOS Terms of Use

**Status:** pilot template. Replace the placeholder operator identity, address, pricing, support contact, and governing-law details before accepting paying customers.

MboaPOS is a software tool for recording sales, managing stock, and preparing merchant receipts. The merchant remains responsible for product prices, stock accuracy, tax registration, NIU accuracy, receipt content, payment-provider contracts, customer notices, backups, and compliance with applicable Cameroon law.

Offline sales are stored on the device and marked pending until synchronization is confirmed. MboaPOS must not be represented as confirming a mobile-money payment until the payment provider returns a verified confirmation. Completed transactions must not be edited; corrections require an auditable void or refund workflow.

The operator must provide support, service availability information, data-export procedures, incident contacts, subscription pricing, cancellation terms, and a data-processing agreement where required. These terms do not replace those documents.
